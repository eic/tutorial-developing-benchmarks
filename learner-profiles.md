---
title: 'Learner Profiles'
---

This lesson targets ePIC collaborators who already have a working analysis and want to turn it into
a benchmark that runs automatically in continuous integration — students, postdocs, and researchers
who have completed the earlier [EIC tutorials](https://eic.github.io/documentation/tutorials.html)
and are comfortable with `eic-shell`, shell scripting, and Git. It assumes familiarity with running
ROOT analysis macros but no prior experience with Snakemake or GitLab CI.
